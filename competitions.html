Process

How It Works

Four steps from registration to results.

01

Register

Fill out the form below and pay the $20 fee. You'll get a confirmation email with all details within 24 hours.

02

Prepare

Use our guidelines to prepare your submission or presentation. Our team is available by email for questions.

03

Submit

Upload your work via the submission portal before the deadline. Late entries are not accepted.

04

Results

Judges score all entries. Winners are announced publicly. Every participant receives an official certificate.
