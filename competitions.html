Process

How It Works

From registration to results — here's what to expect at every CSA competition.

1️⃣

Register

Fill out the registration form and pay the $20 participation fee. You'll receive a confirmation email with all competition details.

2️⃣

Prepare

Use our guidelines and resources to prepare your submission or presentation. Our team is available for questions along the way.

3️⃣

Submit

Upload your work through our submission portal before the deadline. Late submissions are not accepted.

4️⃣

Results & Prizes

Judges review all entries and winners are announced. All participants receive an official certificate. Top performers win cash prizes.
