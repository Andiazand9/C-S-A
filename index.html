Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about CSA and how to get involved.

The Canadian Students Association (CSA) is a national, student-run organization dedicated to providing high-quality academic competitions and enrichment programs for students across Canada. We host events in Entrepreneurship, Business, Science, English, and more.

CSA events are open to all Canadian students at the secondary and post-secondary level. Whether you're in Grade 9 or finishing your undergraduate degree, there's a place for you at CSA.

There is a $20 participation fee per event. This helps cover platform costs and prizes while keeping CSA accessible to as many students as possible. Our team volunteers their time to ensure the fee goes directly toward your experience.

Registration links are posted on our website and Instagram when each event opens. Follow us on social media and turn on notifications so you never miss a new competition.

CSA events are primarily held online to ensure every student across Canada can participate regardless of location. Some regional in-person events may be announced in the future — stay tuned!

Absolutely. We're always looking for motivated students to help organize events, judge competitions, or support marketing and outreach. Reach out to us on Instagram or via email to express your interest.

Why compete with CSA?

Our events are built to challenge you, connect you with peers across Canada, and build skills that go beyond the classroom. Every competition is judge-reviewed and certificate-awarded.

  • National-level competition open to all Canadian students
  • Official certificate of participation for every competitor
  • Cash prizes and recognition for top performers
  • Fully online — compete from anywhere in Canada
  • Real mentorship and feedback from our team
  • Only $20 participation fee per event
